https://github.com/Redth/Maui.Apple.PlatformFeature.Samples (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Jonathan Dick),
see `LICENSE.redth`. Unisono modifies it to route both the Siri App Intents bridge and an in-app MCP server
